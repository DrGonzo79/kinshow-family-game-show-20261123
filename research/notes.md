# Source notes

## Report identity

- **Idea:** SaaS platform that turns family stories into ready-to-host game shows for milestone celebrations
- **Report date:** 2026-11-23
- **Public URL:** https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today
- **IB Index:** 6.0
- **Type / ceiling:** service; $220K-$260K ARR ceiling
- **Year one:** $60K-$90K
- **Difficulty / capital / first revenue:** moderate; $2K-$8K; 30-60 days

## Customer and pain

The buyer is the family organizer who volunteered to entertain 50-100 guests at a milestone birthday, anniversary, or reunion. Their status quo is an exhausted late-night hunt for dated PowerPoint templates, followed by invented questions and uncertain hosting. Event planners and venues are repeat referral channels.

## Wedge and why now

The wedge is a finished, rehearsed production—not a generic quiz builder: approved family stories become three paced rounds, a host script, transitions, and a family-run slide deck. The report argues the timing rests on roughly four million boomers turning 80 in 2026 and low-cost transcription making long interviews economically tractable.

## Demand and market evidence

- “birthday party games”: 18.1K monthly searches; “trivia games”: 90.5K and +50%; report headline aggregate: 4,400/month, +23% YoY.
- Direct done-for-you keywords are reportedly zero-volume, so SEO is not a credible acquisition wedge.
- Here's Your Host is the direct incumbent: $350+ custom shows and 450+ hosted events.
- Etsy anchors personalized trivia from roughly $3-$100; PlayWiwo sells self-serve at $19; live game-show hosts listed by The Bash average roughly $533-$858 in cited cities.
- Pain receipts came from r/partyplanning, r/gameshow, r/trivia, and r/MomForAMinute.

## Offer ladder and economics

Free five-question sample → $29 Starter Trivia PDF → $249 Signature Show → $399 rehearsal package. The core includes a 20-30 minute, three-round show, full host script, slide deck, and seven-day turnaround. The report's 90-day model assumes 32 paid orders, $180 blended AOV, and $5,760 revenue. The decisive validation is 10 orders at $200+ AOV within 90 days.

## GTM

Start with useful Reddit replies, an Etsy listing, and venue/event-planner walk-ins. The source proposes 270 Reddit replies, 1,200 Etsy visits, and one venue partner in 90 days; conversion inputs are explicitly assumptions. Ask venue partners for at least two bookings each in 90 days.

## Risks and build implications

Labor gravity is the kill risk: eight hours at $249 falls below $30/hour. Episodic demand makes every family order a new acquisition. Cheap-template anchors demand visible production value. KinShow therefore timestamps story approval, keeps attribution, makes fact-checking explicit, and focuses its prototype on reducing production/rehearsal time.
