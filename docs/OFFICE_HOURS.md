# Office Hours diagnostic

## Specific customer

Primary: the adult daughter, grandson, or nephew personally accountable for entertainment at a 50-100 guest 70th/80th birthday or anniversary within the next 30 days. Referral ICP: independent venues and event planners serving these events repeatedly.

## Painful status quo and desperate use case

The organizer has promised something personal but is still assembling a dated template after 11 p.m. They need a 20-30 minute activity that works for mixed ages, honors the guest accurately, and can be hosted confidently from a laptop. The desperation is deadline-bound and reputational: a flat room embarrasses the organizer in front of the family.

## Narrowest paid wedge

A **$249 Signature Show**: three produced rounds based on approved stories, a host script with transitions/timing, and a 30-minute rehearsal, delivered in seven days. It wins only if the buyer experiences editorial judgment and hosting confidence—not “AI quiz generation.” Do not start with recurring SaaS.

## Observation and validation

Recruit ten organizers with an event inside 30 days through venue partners, Etsy intent, and high-context Reddit replies. Concierge-produce a free five-question sample from one approved story, then request full $249 payment. Observe story collection, approval revisions, production minutes, rehearsal confusion, and in-room completion. Pass: 10 orders at $200+ AOV in 90 days, median operator time under five hours, at least three venue referrals, and fewer than 10% fact corrections after approval. Kill or reprice if fewer than three buyers pay after 30 qualified samples or median labor exceeds seven hours.

## Future-fit

Software should first compress intake, permissioning, fact checks, run-of-show assembly, and versioning for a skilled producer. Only after repeat service delivery should self-serve tooling emerge. The durable asset is a permissioned production workflow and venue channel, not generic question generation.
