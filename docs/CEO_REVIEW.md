# CEO review

## Challenge

The source calls this SaaS, but the evidence supports a craft service. Families buy once; generic quiz tools are cheap; direct search demand is zero. A self-serve builder would discard the only defensible value—editorial judgment, fact safety, and host confidence. The first product is an operating system for a producer, not a subscription for families.

The $249 wedge is plausible because a direct competitor charges $350+, but it fails if delivery takes eight hours or paid acquisition is required. Venue referrals are a thesis, not evidence. The prototype also cannot prove that questions play well in a room; it only tests organizer comprehension.

## Decisions after review

- Reframed the MVP around story approval, attribution, host cues, rehearsal, and time compression instead of AI magic.
- Made two approved stories a hard minimum and exposed hold/error states.
- Added host mode and a rehearsal checklist because live execution—not content generation—is the outcome.
- Kept deterministic generation and labeled all fixtures fictional; the backend requires human fact checking.
- Set falsifiable gates: 10 orders at $200+ AOV in 90 days, under five median operator hours, three venue referrals, and low post-approval correction.

## GTM and moat verdict

Do not pursue broad SEO or build a marketplace. Start with concierge samples in high-context communities and three local venue/planner partners. The moat is distribution plus a permissioned production system informed by actual room outcomes. If venue referrals fail, this is a small Etsy-adjacent service and should not receive SaaS investment.

## Failure modes and observability

Track consent gaps, fact corrections, stalled relatives, revision count, minutes by production stage, rehearsal no-shows, room/AV problems, conversion by source, and refunds. A show must degrade to an offline PDF/slide deck. Never allow a model to invent survey results, family facts, identities, or sensitive anecdotes. A privacy incident or repeated factual embarrassment is a stop condition.
