# Opportunity score

Weekly-winner contract: `pain × 6 + willingness to pay × 5 + evidence quality × 4 + distribution fit × 3 + CTA testability × 2`, out of 100. Each dimension is 0-5.

| Dimension | Score | Evidence and uncertainty |
|---|---:|---|
| Pain | 3.5 | Deadline-bound family organizers describe hours lost to dated templates and difficult large-room logistics. Severity is credible, but repeat frequency per family is very low. |
| Willingness to pay | 3.5 | Here's Your Host reportedly starts at $350 with 450+ shows; live hosts average hundreds per booking and party entertainment receives 10-20% of budget. The specific $249 family-run package has no payment proof. |
| Evidence quality | 4.0 | Named competitors, public prices, reviews/sales, demographic timing, search data, and direct community receipts triangulate the market. Several figures are secondary/report-derived and none are a KinShow buyer interview. |
| Distribution fit | 3.0 | Etsy, Reddit, venues, and planners are concrete channels, but direct service search is zero and partner sales demand field work. |
| CTA testability | 5.0 | One story can produce a free sample within a day; an upcoming event date and $249 checkout make intent measurable without software or paid APIs. |

**Weighted total:** `(3.5×6) + (3.5×5) + (4×4) + (3×3) + (5×2) = 73.5 / 100`.

Confidence is medium-low. The service tier and pain have credible analogs; offer conversion, labor time, venue referrals, and repeatable acquisition remain assumptions.
